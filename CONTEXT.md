# AI Voice Receptionist

A show-off demo of an AI that answers the phone for a small business. A Visitor
places a Test Call and watches what the Receptionist hears, captures and books.

## Language

**Visitor**:
A person who opens the demo from a link. During a Test Call, the Visitor plays a
customer of the Sample Business.
_Avoid_: User, guest, customer

**Sample Business**:
The made-up business the Receptionist answers for. It is in a niche TEKGUYZ
sells to. It is never a real business.
_Avoid_: Client, company, tenant

**Receptionist**:
The AI voice agent that answers calls for the Sample Business.
_Avoid_: Assistant, bot, agent, Sarah, Viora

**Test Call**:
A spoken call from the Visitor to the Receptionist, made in the browser with
the microphone. No phone number is dialled.
_Avoid_: Demo call, live call, outbound call

**Sample Call**:
A recorded call that plays on the same screens as a Test Call. It plays when a
Test Call cannot happen, for example at a call limit or with no microphone.
It is one real Test Call that the founder made and recorded on purpose.
_Avoid_: Scripted phone, simulator, fake call

**Call Notes**:
What the Receptionist captured from one call: a summary, the caller's details,
the job, the urgency and any booked time.
_Avoid_: Call log, lead record, report

**Owner**:
The owner of the Sample Business. The Dashboard is the Owner's view.
_Avoid_: Admin, business user

**Dashboard**:
The Owner's view of every call the Receptionist handled, with totals and
recent Call Notes.
_Avoid_: Home, overview
