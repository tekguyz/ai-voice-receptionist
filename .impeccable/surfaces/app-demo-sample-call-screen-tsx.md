# Demo call screens (Sample Call now; Test Call, live call, Call Notes later)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a South Florida trade owner on a phone, between jobs, often outdoors in bright daylight. Job: watch the Receptionist take a real service call and see the Owner's side. Action: play the call (later: Call now). Proof: the call itself, the captured details, the booking, the Call Notes. Constraints: PRODUCT.md (no purple, not sci-fi, not cute, honest demo, WCAG 2.2 AA, reduced motion).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The work order writes itself while the caller talks. Refuses the category default of a dark screen, a glowing orb and soft chat bubbles.

OWN-WORLD: A carbon-copy service ticket. The page ground is the canary-yellow copy; the white top sheet lies on it. Every printed part of the form (header, field labels, rules, ticket number, the stamp button) is red form ink. Everything the Receptionist captures is carbon-blue handwriting on the field rules. Transcript text is plain black print. Condensed industrial caps for the form, a plain workhorse sans for reading, one ballpoint hand for captured values. Square corners, ruled lines, a perforated tear line, no cards, no shadows beyond the sheet lying on the copy.

STORY: The Visitor sees an empty work order for the Sample Business, presses the red stamp, and watches each field get written in blue as it is said. When the call ends, the white sheet comes away and the yellow Owner's copy is the Call Notes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: red header band (business, WORK ORDER, ticket number), the line "Your receptionist is standing by", the empty fields as ruled lines, and the red stamp button within thumb reach. Desktop: the call column in the middle at phone width; the fields sheet beside it.

FORM: Service Ticket, grounded list position 1 (taken as Impeccable's pick over the roll), seed key 7d1bb575. Signature interaction: a captured value is written onto its rule left to right, like a pen; reduced motion shows it at once. Motion grammar: one write-in per detail, one sheet lift at the end, nothing else moves.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
